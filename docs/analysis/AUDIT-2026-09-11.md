## The one error found, and what it was

Three documents stated that microphone position −72° and position +54° are "9.3 dB apart at
794 Hz" and called it the largest **broadband** feature. The number was real but it came from a
**mixed-band** fit, not from the tone-notched broadband map. Recomputed:

| map | −72° vs +54° at 794 Hz | largest within-group contrast anywhere |
|---|---|---|
| broadband, tones notched | 2.87 dB | 4.34 dB, −72° vs +54° at 397 Hz |
| mixed band, as the one-pager plots | 9.31 dB | 12.46 dB, −36° vs +36° at 250 Hz |

Corrected in the remedies report, the guide and `CLAUDE.md` to quote both maps with the right
label. The argument the number was supporting is unaffected and in fact stronger: the single
largest feature on the one-pager's map, the 250 Hz tone hole at ±36°, sits **inside** one
position/microphone group and is therefore fully determined despite the degeneracy of §Problem 6.
